The company

We do Christmas lights, and only Christmas lights.

KC Light Co. is a full-service holiday lighting company. We measure every gable, peak and run, cut warm-white C9 lines to the exact length of your roof, and clip them straight so the house reads clean from the street. You get the glow without the ladder.

    Design

    A lighting plan drawn to your home's architecture: rooflines, peaks, windows, trees and walkways.

  • ii.

    Install

    Commercial-grade LED on custom-cut wire, hung by our own crew. No staples, no damage.

  • iii.

    Maintain

    A bulb out or a timer off? We come back and fix it for the rest of the season.

  • iv.

    Remove & store

    After the holidays we take everything down, label it and store it until next year.
